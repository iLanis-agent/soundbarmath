Soundbar Math

The box says three hundred watts, and your ears were always going to hear thirty.

Boundary: illustrative arithmetic only Soundbar Math keeps labeled-norm arithmetic only. The peak-to-RMS ratio, the room classes and the decibel steps are labeled norms, not measurements of your soundbar, your room or your ears. Trust your own listening, follow the maker's RMS figure over anything this page says, and protect your hearing at high volume.

The peak truth

The big number is peak marketing power. Honest RMS runs about a tenth of it: an 800-watt box is 80.0 watts RMS honestly.

The doubling truth

Doubling the watts buys 3.0 dB - barely audible. Ten times the watts is about twice as loud. The 600-watt model was never going to blow away the 300.

The room truth

A 400-square-foot room asks the large 100-watt class; 30.0 honest watts runs 70.0 watts short. The room, not the box, sets the bill.
